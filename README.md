# Lane Clash

A one-screen mobile lane shooter: hold the horde back while you grab upgrades.

## How to play

Open `index.html` on a phone or in a desktop browser. Choose Normal, Hard, or Insane, then tap to start. The choice is saved on this device, and each difficulty keeps its own best score.

- Drag, or tap the left or right half of the screen, to switch lanes. Arrow keys, A/D, and 1/2 work on a keyboard during a run. On the menu, 1/2/3 pick Normal/Hard/Insane.
- Your squad fires on its own.
- The green upgrade lane has gates. Walk through a gate to apply it, and shoot a gate to raise its number. Red gates hurt you.
- The red horde lane sends monsters. Anything that reaches the red line costs HP.
- Balance grabbing upgrades with holding the horde back. Lanes swap every few waves, and a boss arrives every 5 waves.
- After you are overrun, pick the difficulty for the next run and tap to play again. The run you are in does not change difficulty.

Normal is a step up from the original release. Hard and Insane raise horde pressure from that baseline.
