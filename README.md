# Lane Clash

A one-screen mobile lane shooter: hold the horde back while you grab upgrades.

## How to play

Open `index.html` on a phone or in a desktop browser. Choose Normal, Hard, or Crazy, then tap to start. The choice is saved on this device, and each difficulty keeps its own best score.

- Drag, or tap the left or right half of the screen, to switch lanes. Arrow keys, A/D, and 1/2 work on a keyboard during a run. On the menu, 1/2/3 pick Normal/Hard/Crazy.
- Your squad fires on its own.
- The green upgrade lane has gates. Walk through a gate to apply it, and shoot a gate to raise its number. Red gates hurt you.
- Purple gates are rarer power-ups: pierce, ricochet, split fire, low-HP rage, a pulse shield, slow shots, heal-on-kill, a gate magnet, a charge blast, empowered gates, a short overdrive, and one second wind.
- The red horde lane sends monsters. Anything that reaches the red line costs HP. Normal already sends more enemies than the original release. Hard and Crazy make that horde denser, faster, and tougher.
- Lanes swap every few waves, and a boss arrives every 5 waves.
- After you are overrun, pick the difficulty for the next run and tap to play again.
